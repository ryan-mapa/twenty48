    HOW TO PLAY

    • Arrow keys, or swipe on a phone. Every move slides all the sushi as far as it will go.
    • Two of the same sushi merge into the next one up — that's your score.
    • Two uni cancel each other out for 8192 points, clearing space. There's no end!
    • When you have no more moves, the game ends. Post your score under any name — sign in with Google to keep that name yours.
